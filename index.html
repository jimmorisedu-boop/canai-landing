Your work should not live in someone else’s tab

Most AI tools run far away from you. Your prompts, uploads and results sit in a queue you cannot see, priced by a plan you cannot change. CanAI flips that around.

In a browser tab

  • Your prompts sit in someone else’s queue
  • A subscription decides your limits
  • Your files leave before you see them
  • One dead tab can lose an evening

On your desk

  • Boards, media and settings live in one folder
  • Your own fal.ai key at fal.ai prices
  • Renders continue with the tab closed
  • Automatic backups of every board

What that means in practice

One tool for the whole job, from the first reference to the finished board.

Local by default

Boards, media and settings live in one folder. Back it up, move it to another computer, or delete it and nothing remains anywhere.

One canvas for the whole job

References, drafts, video and copy sit side by side. Sketch over a photo, crop a frame, and send the result straight into the next prompt.

Their prices, not a markup

Your own fal.ai key, and an estimated price for every run before you press Generate. No seats, no tiers, no subscription.

Long jobs keep running

Close the tab, take the iPad, shut the lid. Video renders continue inside the app and land on the board when you come back.

The studio follows you

Turn on tablet access and open the same boards from an iPad over your home network, while the computer does the heavy work. Photos and previews are sized for the tablet screen.

Your boards, your media and your key stay in one folder on your machine, while the models do the heavy lifting.

Three steps and you are generating

  1. 01

    Unzip and run

    The launcher brings its own runtime, installs nothing into your system and opens the studio in your browser.

  2. 02

    Add your fal.ai key

    Paste it once in Settings. It is stored on your machine and used only for your own requests.

  3. 03

    Make something

    Drop a photo on the canvas, sketch over it, pick a model and press Generate. The result lands next to your reference.
